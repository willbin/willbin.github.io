千岛隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

概要

千岛（Qiandao）由 Wecomic 提供，是一款本地有声书播放器，无需注册账号。当前版本不包含广告、用户追踪或第三方分析服务，不向开发者的服务器收集或上传个人信息。

保存在设备上的数据

应用使用设备本地存储保存当前集数、播放进度、倍速和播放模式。这些数据仅用于继续收听和恢复偏好设置，不会发送给开发者。音频随应用内置，在设备本地播放。卸载应用会移除本机应用数据；系统备份由你的 Apple 设备和备份设置管理。

系统功能与外部链接

应用使用 Apple 音频和锁屏媒体功能显示当前内容并控制播放，不请求麦克风、通讯录、照片或定位权限。只有你主动打开支持或隐私网页、或发送支持邮件时，才会离开应用。网页托管于 GitHub Pages，访问网页时 GitHub 可能按其隐私声明处理常规访问日志；这与应用内的本地播放数据分开。

联系支持

你主动发送的邮件和附件会由邮箱服务处理，仅用于回复与解决问题。请勿发送与问题无关的敏感信息。你可通过邮件请求删除你向开发者提供的支持信息。

政策更新

应用的数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新此页面及生效日期。

Qiandao Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026.

Qiandao is an offline audiobook player by Wecomic. No account is required. The current version contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics and does not collect or upload personal information to the developer's servers.

On-device information

The app saves the current episode, listening position, playback speed, and playback mode locally to restore your listening session. Bundled audio is played locally. Uninstalling removes the app's local data; Apple device backups are controlled by your device and backup settings.

System features and external links

The app uses Apple's audio and lock-screen media controls. It does not request access to the microphone, contacts, photos, or location. Opening a support or privacy webpage is your choice. These webpages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process normal access logs under GitHub's privacy statement. App listening data is not sent with these visits.

Support and contact

Information you choose to email is used to respond to your request. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information. To request deletion of support information you provided, contact wei.you.bin@163.com. We update this policy when the app's data practices change.
