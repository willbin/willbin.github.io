WeConverter

Local conversion for everyday files.

Create PDFs from photos, compress images and videos, convert image formats, and extract audio without creating an account.

本地完成照片转 PDF、压缩图片、转换图片格式、压缩视频和提取声音，无需注册账号。

View on App Store Contact Support

Five common tools

  • Photos to PDF
  • Compress image
  • JPG and PNG conversion
  • Compress video
  • Extract video audio as M4A

On-device workflow

Files are imported from Photos and processed on the device. Results are saved or shared using the iOS system share sheet.

Privacy

The app does not require an account. Media selected by the user is processed locally. The app uses Google AdMob to display ads, and AdMob may collect data described in the App Store privacy label.

核心功能

  • 多张照片合成为 PDF
  • 压缩图片并导出 JPG
  • JPG / PNG 图片格式转换
  • 压缩视频文件大小
  • 从视频中提取 M4A 声音文件

隐私与支持

用户选择的媒体文件在设备本地处理，不需要登录账号。应用使用 Google AdMob 展示广告，广告 SDK 可能收集 App Store 隐私标签中披露的数据。
